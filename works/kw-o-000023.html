About the work / 作品介绍

Weightless Dance began with a plastic bag caught in the wind. Creases and translucent folds register a moment in which the film unfurls and turns in the air. The changing shape of this ordinary object makes the invisible movement of the wind perceptible.

《轻舞》起于一只被风吹动的塑料袋。薄膜的折痕与透光处显出它在空中舒展、转向的瞬间。肉眼看不见的风，通过这个日常物件的形态变化显露出来。
