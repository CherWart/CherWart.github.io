Painting as a Field of Relation绘画作为关系之域

Kaixuan Wang works across watercolor, oil painting and printmaking, exploring changing environments, material traces, living presence, and the relationships that emerge between artist, matter and world.

王凯萱以水彩、油画与版画进行创作，持续关注变化中的环境、物质留下的痕迹与生命的在场，并探索艺术家、材料与世界之间不断发生的关系。

About关于

Kaixuan Wang王凯萱

Kaixuan Wang is a Chinese contemporary artist whose practice is rooted in painting and extends across watercolor, oil and printmaking. Her work moves between observed reality and increasingly open forms of painting, with sustained attention to environment, material, transformation and lived experience.

王凯萱是一位中国当代艺术家，创作以绘画为核心，并延伸至水彩、油画与版画。她的实践往返于现实观察与逐渐开放的绘画语言之间，持续关注环境、物质、变化与生命经验。
