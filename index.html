Artistic Practice and Thought艺术实践与思想

KAIXUAN WANG is a contemporary Chinese artist based in Shenzhen whose primary practice is painting. Watercolor has long been her central medium, alongside an ongoing engagement with oil and other forms of painterly experimentation. Beginning with direct experience of real environments, her work considers urban change, constructed structures, nature, matter, everyday life, and the shifting relations between living beings and the surrounding world.

She is especially attentive to things at the edges of everyday vision. Through active selection, scale, color, composition, space, and the flow and sedimentation inherent to water-based materials, she reorganizes observed reality into painted spaces with psychological tension and material presence. Figuration provides an important foundation for her practice, while expressive, abstract, and materially generated visual elements continue to enter the work. Across these forms runs a sustained inquiry into seeing, life, matter, environment, and the relation between people and the world.

She also maintains a practice of theoretical writing, developing questions arising from painting into further reflections on art, life, and participation. Theory is not an instruction manual for the work, but another path of thought evolving alongside the paintings.

王凯萱是一位以绘画为主要实践的中国当代艺术家，目前生活和工作于深圳。水彩是她长期使用的核心媒介，同时她也持续进行油画及其他绘画实验。

她的创作从现实环境和直接经验出发，关注城市变化、人工结构、自然、物质、日常生活以及生命与周围世界之间不断变化的关系。她尤其关注那些处于日常视觉边缘的事物。通过主动选择、尺度、色彩、构图、空间以及水性材料自身的流动与沉积，她将观察到的现实重新组织为具有心理张力和物质感的绘画空间。

她的实践以具象绘画为重要基础，同时不断吸收表现性、抽象性和材料自身产生的视觉因素。不同形式背后持续存在的，是她对观看、生命、物质、环境以及人与世界关系的研究。她同时持续进行艺术理论写作，将绘画实践中产生的问题进一步发展为关于艺术、生命与参与的思考。理论并不是作品的说明书，而是与绘画实践并行发展的另一条思考路径。
