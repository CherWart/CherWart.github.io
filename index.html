Artistic Practice and Thought艺术实践与思想

KAIXUAN WANG is a contemporary Chinese visual artist and independent theorist. Her art philosophy emerges from sustained painting practice and continually returns to reshape her ways of seeing, choosing materials, and making work. Through the interconnected inquiries developed in her three books, she considers art as a way life enters into relation, softness as a non-confrontational relational capacity that sustains connection across difference, and the human being as one participant among all things—retaining agency and responsibility without occupying the center. Working across watercolor, oil painting, and printmaking, she transforms encounters with nature, everyday perception, space, and traces of time into visual fields with philosophical resonance.

KAIXUAN WANG / 王凯萱是一位中国当代视觉艺术家与独立理论作者。她的艺术哲学源于长期绘画实践，又持续返回作品，改变她的观看、材料选择与创作方式。围绕《生命艺术论》《柔性本元》与《一个参与者》，她探讨艺术如何成为生命建立关系的方式，柔性如何作为非对抗的关系能力维持差异中的连接，以及人如何作为万物中的一个参与者承担主体性与责任。她以水彩、油画与版画为主要媒介，将自然经验、日常感知、空间与时间痕迹转化为具有哲学指向的视觉场域。

Her practice began with rigorous academic training in drawing, oil painting, Chinese ink, printmaking, mixed media, structure, color, and representation. In 2019, she turned decisively toward watercolor, using its transparency, fluidity, and irreversibility to move from technical mastery toward a more open inquiry into presence, time, and transformation.

她的创作道路始于严格的学院训练，涵盖素描、油画、中国画、版画、综合材料、结构、色彩与造型。2019年，她明确转向水彩，并以水彩的透明性、流动性与不可逆性，将技术训练推进为关于存在、时间与转化的开放性研究。

Her philosophical concepts do not precede the paintings as explanations to be illustrated. They arise from encounters with watercolor, light, places, materials, and lived experience; once articulated, they return to the studio as questions for the next stage of painting. Practice and thought therefore generate and correct one another.

她的哲学概念并非先于绘画、再由作品加以说明。概念从水彩、光、场所、材料与生命经验的相遇中产生；在被表达之后，它们又返回工作室，成为下一阶段绘画需要面对的问题。实践与思想由此相互生成、相互校正。

Within this reciprocal process, softness is not passivity or retreat. It names a capacity to receive pressure, preserve relation, adjust without erasing difference, and sustain life through change. The framework remains accountable to what painting reveals, resists, or disproves.

在这一往返过程中，“柔性”并非被动或退让，而是承受压力、维系关系、在不抹平差异的前提下调节，并使生命在变化中延续的能力。理论始终需要接受绘画经验的检验、修正与反证。

Research / Philosophy研究 / 理论

From Soft Her Power to Soft Origin 从“柔性她力量”到“柔性本元”

KAIXUAN WANG's philosophical vocabulary grows from painting rather than preceding it. Watercolor's transparency and irreversibility, the weight of infrastructure, traces of labor, and changes in land and light produce questions about relation, time, and coexistence.

王凯萱的哲学词汇从绘画经验中生长，而非先于绘画成立。水彩的透明与不可逆、基础设施的重量、劳动留下的痕迹，以及土地与光的变化，共同提出关于关系、时间与共存的问题。

When these questions become concepts, they return to the studio. A concept must remain open to correction by color, material, observation, and the resistance of an actual image. Painting and philosophy thus operate as a reciprocal method: each gives the other direction, limits, and evidence.

当这些问题被表达为概念，它们又返回工作室。概念必须接受色彩、材料、观察与具体图像阻力的校正。绘画与哲学因此构成一种往返的方法：彼此提供方向，也彼此限定并提供证据。

Softness, in this inquiry, is a relational capacity rather than a promise of harmony. It describes how life can receive pressure, maintain difference, adjust, and continue without making domination its only measure of strength. The inquiry remains grounded in concrete subjects: land, labor, infrastructure, memory, daily life, and shared environments.

在这一研究中，“柔性”是一种关系能力，而不是对和谐结果的承诺。它描述生命如何承受压力、保留差异、作出调节，并在不把支配作为唯一力量尺度的情况下继续存在。研究始终落实于土地、劳动、基础设施、记忆、日常生活与共享环境等具体对象。
