KAIXUANWANG 王凯萱

I paint what is seen, yet often not truly observed.

I begin with the world as encountered, attending to fields, matter, structures, and traces of life that readily slip from view within everyday experience.

What draws me is not appearance alone, but the ways different things coexist, act upon one another, and together shape the reality we inhabit.

For me, painting is a way of entering these relations.

绘画那些被看见，却常常未被真正观看的事物。

我从现实世界出发，关注日常经验中容易被忽略的场域、物质、结构与生命痕迹。

吸引我的并不只是它们的外观，而是不同事物如何同时存在、彼此影响，并共同构成我们所处的现实。

绘画对我而言，是进入这种关系的一种方式。

Artistic Practice and Thought艺术实践与思想

KAIXUAN WANG is a contemporary Chinese artist based in Shenzhen whose primary practice is painting. Watercolor has long been her central medium, alongside an ongoing engagement with oil and other forms of painterly experimentation. Beginning with direct experience of real environments, her work considers urban change, constructed structures, nature, matter, everyday life, and the shifting relations between living beings and the surrounding world.

She is especially attentive to things at the edges of everyday vision. Through active selection, scale, color, composition, space, and the flow and sedimentation inherent to water-based materials, she reorganizes observed reality into painted spaces with psychological tension and material presence. Figuration provides an important foundation for her practice, while expressive, abstract, and materially generated visual elements continue to enter the work. Across these forms runs a sustained inquiry into seeing, life, matter, environment, and the relation between people and the world.

She also maintains a practice of theoretical writing, developing questions arising from painting into further reflections on art, life, and participation. Theory is not an instruction manual for the work, but another path of thought evolving alongside the paintings.

王凯萱是一位以绘画为主要实践的中国当代艺术家，目前生活和工作于深圳。水彩是她长期使用的核心媒介，同时她也持续进行油画及其他绘画实验。

她的创作从现实环境和直接经验出发，关注城市变化、人工结构、自然、物质、日常生活以及生命与周围世界之间不断变化的关系。她尤其关注那些处于日常视觉边缘的事物。通过主动选择、尺度、色彩、构图、空间以及水性材料自身的流动与沉积，她将观察到的现实重新组织为具有心理张力和物质感的绘画空间。

她的实践以具象绘画为重要基础，同时不断吸收表现性、抽象性和材料自身产生的视觉因素。不同形式背后持续存在的，是她对观看、生命、物质、环境以及人与世界关系的研究。她同时持续进行艺术理论写作，将绘画实践中产生的问题进一步发展为关于艺术、生命与参与的思考。理论并不是作品的说明书，而是与绘画实践并行发展的另一条思考路径。

Artist Statement艺术家陈述

My painting begins with looking at the real.

I am often drawn to things that are easily overlooked: urban spaces in transition, constructed structures, old walls and roads, machinery, plants, bodies of water, and the many traces left by everyday life. They may not naturally belong to the traditional idea of a “painterly subject,” yet when I stop to look, I find within them relations that are both complex and specific.

This way of looking determines both why I paint and how I paint.

For me, figuration does not mean reproducing reality. What enters the image and what is left out; what is enlarged or compressed; and how color, scale, space, and material reorganize the real—these are themselves the language of painting. I ask whether reality, once it has passed through painting, can reveal what habit had previously obscured.

My work therefore returns to relations between the individual and the environment, the constructed and the natural, matter and life, order and chance, change and trace.

Watercolor has long been my primary medium. The flow, permeation, and sedimentation produced by water, pigment, and paper—and their changes that can never be entirely predicted—mean that painting is more than the execution of a predetermined image. I retain judgment and choice while allowing material and process to genuinely affect how a work takes form.

In recent years I have also been opening the boundaries between figuration, expression, and abstraction. I do not understand them as mutually exclusive artistic directions. For me, they are different forms painting may take when it encounters different kinds of experience.

These practices continue to point toward one question: when the world is no longer merely an object to be observed and depicted, how might we understand the way we coexist with the things around us?

Painting is my continuing way of entering this question.

我的绘画始于对现实的观看。

我经常被一些容易被忽略的事物吸引：正在变化的城市空间、人工结构、旧墙和道路、机器、植物、水面，以及日常生活留下的各种痕迹。它们未必天然属于传统意义上的“绘画题材”，但当我停下来观看时，我会发现其中存在着复杂而具体的关系。

这种观看决定了我为什么画，也决定了我如何画。

对我而言，具象并不意味着复制现实。选择什么进入画面、什么被舍弃，什么被放大或压缩，色彩、尺度、空间和材料如何重新组织现实，这些本身就是绘画语言。我关心的是：现实经过绘画之后，能否显露出那些原本被习惯遮蔽的东西。

因此，我的作品反复涉及个体与环境、人工与自然、物质与生命、秩序与偶然、变化与痕迹之间的关系。

水彩是我长期使用的主要媒介。水、颜料和纸张所产生的流动、渗透、沉积以及不可完全预知的变化，使绘画不只是预先设定图像的执行过程。我保持判断和选择，同时允许材料和过程真正影响作品的形成。

近年来，我也在进一步打开具象、表现性与抽象之间的边界。我并不把它们理解为彼此排斥的艺术方向。对我而言，它们只是绘画面对不同经验时可能采取的不同形式。

这些实践最终持续指向一个问题：当世界不再只是被观看和描绘的对象，我们如何理解自己与周围事物共同存在的方式？

绘画是我持续进入这个问题的方法。

Selected Works 精选作品

These works were made in different places and at different times, yet they share a sustained way of looking. Construction sites, industrial structures, roads, plants, water, old walls, lived environments, and the various traces left by reality do not appear in my paintings as isolated subjects. I am interested in how they together constitute the world we inhabit, and how different forms of life, matter, and environment act upon one another within it. Choosing what to paint is itself part of my practice.

这些作品产生于不同地点和不同时间，却共享一种持续的观看方式。城市建设、工业结构、道路、植物、水、旧墙、生活环境以及现实留下的各种痕迹，在我的绘画中并不是彼此孤立的题材。我关注的是它们如何共同构成我们正在生活的世界，以及不同生命、物质和环境如何在其中彼此作用。选择画什么，本身就是我的创作的一部分。

01

TRANSFORMING FIELDS

变化中的场域

Environments in transformation: construction, infrastructure, industrial structures, roads, architecture, artificial coverings, spatial change, and sites where natural and constructed forces meet.

关注现实环境正在发生变化的状态：城市建设、基础设施、工业结构、道路、建筑、人工覆盖、空间改造，以及自然与人工环境发生改变的现场。

02

MATTER & TRACE

物质与痕迹

Matter itself and the traces left by time, environment, and use: water, walls, vessels, plants, land, surfaces, reflections, deposits, coverings, erosion, residue, and texture.

关注物质本身，以及时间、环境和使用留下的痕迹：水、墙面、容器、植物、土地、表面、反射、沉积、覆盖、侵蚀、残留与纹理。

03

LIVING PRESENCE

生命在场

How life exists within an environment: human presence at environmental scale, traces of labor, plant and natural life, and the ways life appears, continues, or leaves a mark.

关注生命如何存在于环境之中：人在巨大环境中的存在、劳动留下的生命痕迹、植物与自然生命，以及生命如何出现、持续或留下痕迹。

04

OPEN PAINTING

开放的绘画

An experimental space in which lived reality continues to transform through painting, opening the boundary between figuration and abstraction and giving color, structure, material, and painterly action greater autonomy.

绘画语言继续向外发展的实验空间：现实经验在绘画中进一步转化，具象与抽象之间的边界被打开，色彩、结构、材料和绘画行为获得更大的自主性。

Writing & Ideas写作与观念

Questions Emerging from Painting 从绘画中产生的问题

My theoretical writing grows from questions that recur throughout a sustained painting practice: How does an artist enter the world? Is material merely a tool to be used? What does it mean to look? How do people and the things around them affect one another? While remaining open to the influence of environment, material, and chance, how can a person retain choice, judgment, and responsibility?

These questions have gradually developed into an ongoing inquiry into life, participation, and artistic practice. Theory and painting are related, but theory is not a prerequisite for encountering the work.

我的理论写作产生于长期绘画实践中反复出现的问题：艺术家如何进入世界？材料是否只是被使用的工具？观看意味着什么？人与周围事物如何彼此影响？人在接受环境、材料和偶然影响的同时，如何保持选择、判断与责任？

这些问题逐渐形成了我关于生命、参与和艺术实践的持续研究。理论与绘画彼此关联，但理论不是观看作品的前提。
