Practice

KAIXUAN WANG is a contemporary visual artist based in Shenzhen, China, whose long-term practice moves across watercolor, oil painting, printmaking, and mixed media.

KAIXUAN WANG / 王凯萱是常驻中国深圳的当代视觉艺术家，其长期实践跨越水彩、油画、版画与综合材料。

Rather than developing through a linear progression from one medium or style to another, her work has evolved through a continuous inquiry into perception, material, life, relation, and the position of the human being within the world. This underlying concern can be traced through her practice from her earlier works to the present, while becoming increasingly conscious and articulated over time.

她的创作并非沿着一种媒介或风格向另一种媒介或风格线性推进，而是在对感知、材料、生命、关系以及人在世界中的位置的持续追问中发展。这一内在关切从早期作品延续至今，并在长期实践中逐渐变得更加自觉与明确。

For Wang, the visible world is an entry point rather than the final destination of a painting. Image and thought emerge together. Recognisable forms remain when social, historical, spatial, or material relationships depend upon recognition; at other times, forms loosen when fixed representation begins to constrain bodily experience, perception, memory, psychological states, or the behavior of materials.

对王凯萱而言，可见世界是绘画的入口，而不是作品的最终目的。图像与思想共同生成。当社会、历史、空间或物质关系依赖于辨识时，作品保留可识别的形式；当固定再现开始限制身体经验、感知、记忆、心理状态或材料行为时，形式则会变得松动。

Watercolor, oil painting, and printmaking therefore do not represent separate artistic identities or successive stylistic phases. They are different material conditions chosen according to the questions each work requires.

因此，水彩、油画与版画并不代表彼此分离的艺术身份，也不是先后更替的风格阶段。它们是根据每件作品所要求的问题而选择的不同物质条件。

Watercolor became the most publicly visible part of Wang's practice between approximately 2019 and 2024, particularly through exhibitions and juried competitions, while her broader painting practice continued in parallel. Its transparency, fluidity, and irreversibility also contributed significantly to the development of her thinking about relation, uncertainty, transformation, and softness.

约在2019至2024年间，水彩成为王凯萱实践中最具公共可见度的部分，尤其通过展览与评审竞赛被广泛呈现；与此同时，她更广泛的绘画实践始终并行展开。水彩的透明性、流动性与不可逆性，也对她关于关系、不确定性、转化与柔性的思考产生了重要影响。

Her philosophical writing grows from this sustained artistic practice rather than functioning as a theory imposed upon it. Through concepts including Soft Origin and Artist as Participant, Wang considers the artist not as an external controller of the world, but as one participant within a field of relations—retaining agency, judgment, and responsibility while remaining open to material, environment, other beings, time, and forces beyond complete control.

她的哲学写作生长于这种持续的艺术实践，而不是事后施加于作品之上的理论。通过“柔性本元”与“艺术家作为参与者”等概念，王凯萱把艺术家理解为关系场域中的一个参与者，而非世界的外部控制者：艺术家保有主体性、判断与责任，同时向材料、环境、其他生命、时间以及无法被完全控制的力量保持开放。

Meaning, in this practice, is never entirely closed by the artist. The work establishes conditions for perception, while the viewer participates in its continuing formation.

在这一实践中，意义从不由艺术家彻底封闭。作品为感知建立条件，而观看者参与意义的持续形成。

Research / Philosophy研究 / 理论

From Soft Her Power to Soft Origin 从“柔性她力量”到“柔性本元”

KAIXUAN WANG's philosophical vocabulary grows from painting rather than preceding it. Watercolor's transparency and irreversibility, the weight of infrastructure, traces of labor, and changes in land and light produce questions about relation, time, and coexistence.

王凯萱的哲学词汇从绘画经验中生长，而非先于绘画成立。水彩的透明与不可逆、基础设施的重量、劳动留下的痕迹，以及土地与光的变化，共同提出关于关系、时间与共存的问题。

When these questions become concepts, they return to the studio. A concept must remain open to correction by color, material, observation, and the resistance of an actual image. Painting and philosophy thus operate as a reciprocal method: each gives the other direction, limits, and evidence.

当这些问题被表达为概念，它们又返回工作室。概念必须接受色彩、材料、观察与具体图像阻力的校正。绘画与哲学因此构成一种往返的方法：彼此提供方向，也彼此限定并提供证据。

Softness, in this inquiry, is a relational capacity rather than a promise of harmony. It describes how life can receive pressure, maintain difference, adjust, and continue without making domination its only measure of strength. The inquiry remains grounded in concrete subjects: land, labor, infrastructure, memory, daily life, and shared environments.

在这一研究中，“柔性”是一种关系能力，而不是对和谐结果的承诺。它描述生命如何承受压力、保留差异、作出调节，并在不把支配作为唯一力量尺度的情况下继续存在。研究始终落实于土地、劳动、基础设施、记忆、日常生活与共享环境等具体对象。
